Celebrating our 75th Year

Fossil Threads in the Web of Life

February
Scott Sampson

What's 75 million years old and brand spanking new? A teenage Utahceratops! Come to the Saroyan, armed with your best dinosaur roar, when Scott Sampson, Research Curator at the Utah Museum of Natural History, steps to the podium.

Scott Sampson is a Canadian-born paleontologist who received his Ph.D. in zoology from the University of Toronto. His doctoral work focused on horned dinosaurs from the Late Cretaceous of Montana.

Following graduation in 1993, Sampson worked at the American Museum of Natural History in New York City. He later became an assistant professor and curator of vertebrate paleontology at the Utah Museum of Natural History.

In addition to his museum and laboratory studies, Sampson has conducted paleontological work in Zimbabwe, South Africa, Madagascar, the United States, and Canada. He also hosted Discovery Channel's Dinosaur Planet and completed the book Dinosaur Odyssey: Fossil Threads in the Web of Life.
